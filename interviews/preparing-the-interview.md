# Preparing for the tech interview

### Checklist

##### Before the interview

- Learn more about the job you are applying for and be prepared for the following questions:

```
    [] Why are you interested in the position?
    [] How does the position matches your long-term career goals
    [] What kind of technologies are you interested in
    [] Roles and responsibilities you look for
```

- Prepare a cheat-sheet with your professional background, answers to common questions, vocabulary (if needed). Have your CV close at hand.
 
- Tools prep:

```
    [] Necessary software installed (Zoom, IDE, AI tools, etc)
    [] Internet connection. Speed Test.
    [] Mobile in silent mode
    [] Glass of water
```

- Take note of the interviewer's names

##### During the interview

- Break the ice with a question about the weather, time difference, location, etc.
- When solving practical tasks, make sure you're moving in the right direction always.
  - You should clearly understand which problem you are trying to solve. Ask if you understood the task correctly.
    - Functional questions:
      - Should we consider empty data?
      - Can we have duplicates?
    - Non-functional questions:
      - How much data can we expect?
  - Always tell your assumptions and ask for feedback continuously, then see if it's positive, or you need to change direction.
  - Always think out loud
  - Discuss your solution with the interviewer before implementation
  - Don't build a perfect solution at once. Satisfy given criteria and optimize after.

##### At the end of the interview

- Questions to ask the interviewer:
  - Was there a previous engineer at this position and why he left the team
  - Do you feel happy working in this project?
  - What is the team composition and what are the roles in the team? 
  - What are the project timelines?
  - Do you regularly work / be on call during the weekend?
  - Thank the interviewer for their time.
